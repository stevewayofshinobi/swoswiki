Shinobi Clothing & Quality-of-Life Update

  • Various bug fixes.
  • Shinobi shirt and pants are now one combined outfit.
  • Added five new Shinobi outfits and one windbreaker.
  • Damage dealt by player-controlled units now counts toward jutsu learning requirements.
  • Added Gama Chan, a wallet for storing Ryo savings.
  • Chakra Control now requires at least 5 Chakra to remain active.
  • Inuzuka players can teleport their dog to themselves.
  • Genin name colors now reflect their home village.
  • Byakugo storage now progresses every 10 Minecraft days instead of 25, with less Chakra per diamond.
